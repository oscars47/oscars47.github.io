Research interests

I am a second year DPhil (PhD) student in Atomic and Laser Physics at the University of Oxford, co-supervised by Andrew Daley in the Theory of Quantum Systems group and Bálint Koczor in the Oxford Quantum Information and Computation group.

My research explores how to encode and extract information in quantum many-body systems. My work is theoretical, with recent projects on randomized measurements in systems with limited local control and on digital techniques based on block encoding.

I work closely with experimentalists to test and implement some of these ideas on real devices, including neutral atoms in tweezer arrays (QuEra Computing Inc.) and ultracold atoms in optical lattices (Ulrich Schneider's quasicrystal group). I find that experimental constraints often raise interesting theoretical questions and inspire new, simple approaches to big questions.

My email is oscar.scholin@physics.ox.ac.uk; please reach out with any questions or ideas!
